Practice: FizzBuzz

12 minutes. Write your code inside the script tag at the bottom of this file. (Slide 12)
THE TASK
Print the numbers from 1 to 15 in the console. But:
multiples of 3 → print Fizz
multiples of 5 → print Buzz
multiples of both 3 and 5 → print FizzBuzz
EXPECTED OUTPUT (one per line)
1 2 Fizz 4 Buzz Fizz 7 8 Fizz Buzz 11 Fizz 13 14 FizzBuzz
CONSOLE — press F12 (or right-click, Inspect) and open the Console tab.
Save, reload, and compare your console with the expected output.
